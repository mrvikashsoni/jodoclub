❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about joining, booking, and partnering with Jodo.

What is Jodo Library?

Jodo Library (by Jodo Club) is a tech-enabled aggregator and IoT SaaS platform that connects competitive exam aspirants with verified, silent study spaces. Students can book exact seats like movie tickets, and library owners get automated management software to eliminate paper registers.

How does the "Sell Seat on Absent" feature work?

If you have a monthly subscription and need to travel home for 5-10 days for an exam or festival, you can mark your seat as "vacant" in the app. Jodo makes that slot available for hourly micro-renters or temporary students, and you earn cashback directly to your wallet!

How do Attendance-Based Discounts work?

Whenever you enter the study space, scan your app QR code at the desk or gate. When you maintain high attendance (e.g. 20+ days a month), your next monthly renewal gets an automated discipline discount of up to 25%.

Why is the B2B LMS 100% Free for Library Owners?

Traditional software charges owners monthly fees. Jodo provides free lifetime software to eliminate friction, digitize seat inventory in real-time, and help owners fill empty seats through our consumer marketplace.

Can I book a seat just for 2-3 hours instead of a full month?

Yes! Through our Micro-Renting feature, freelancers, university students, and professionals can walk into any partner library, scan the desk QR code, and pay by the hour.
